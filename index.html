PLEASE READ BEFORE USING

Privacy, storage & responsible use

LOCAL-FIRST
01

Browser-only storage. Records are kept in LocalStorage on this browser and device. They do not sync across users or computers. Clearing browser data may remove them; export backups regularly.

02

No automatic outreach. The app does not call, email or message prospects. It does not scrape contacts or connect to an external CRM.

03

TruthGuard is a phrase flagger. It checks a small configurable list of absolute-claim phrases. It is not an AI fact-checker, legal review or guarantee that other statements are accurate.

04

Use approved information. Do not promise placements, salaries, certifications or outcomes unless the exact claim is approved and supported by official company material.

05

Protect personal information. Use fictional or appropriately authorised demo data. Do not store sensitive personal, financial or student information in this prototype.

DESTRUCTIVE ACTION
